Use Cases • Applications

Typical Applications

Explore where this system delivers the most value in real-world clean environments.

Pharmaceutical Production

Essential for sterile manufacturing, our coving systems ensure no particle entrapment at critical wall-ceiling and wall-floor junctions, meeting GMP standards.

Hygienic Wall Junctions
Seamlessly connects ISO 5 panels with ceiling grids using R50 radius coving.
Airtight Floor Tracks
Adjustable U-channels accommodate uneven floors while maintaining seal integrity.

Bio-Safety Labs (BSL)

Corrosion-resistant anodized profiles designed to withstand frequent sterilization including VHP and UV exposure in BSL-2 and BSL-3 labs.

Chemical Resistance
Tested against Spor-Klenz and heavy oxidative agents.

Operating Theaters

Anti-bacterial coatings and flush installation prevent germ buildup in surgical environments, meeting stringent healthcare standards.

Flush Door Frames
Profile systems integrate perfectly with hermetic doors and viewing panels.
