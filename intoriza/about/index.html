Who We Are

About PakGusu Cleanroom Manufacturer in Pakistan

PakGusu Technology (Pvt) Ltd is a specialized cleanroom solution provider based in Lahore, Pakistan. We design, manufacture, and install modular cleanroom systems that meet international standards for cleanliness, safety, and regulatory compliance.

Historically, critical cleanroom components were imported, leading to long lead times. By establishing local manufacturing in partnership with GUSU Purification, we provide faster delivery and competitive pricing without compromising quality.

Why Choose PakGusu?

  • Global + Local: GUSU China expertise + Lahore manufacturing.
  • Compliance-Driven: ISO & GMP aligned designs.
  • Scalable: Modular systems that grow with you.
  • Support: Dedicated local maintenance & validation team.

OUR STORY & TIMELINE

From Founding to Strategic Partnership

Trace our journey from a small cleanroom specialist to a trusted global partner, through key milestones that shaped who we are today.

Founding: Bridging the Gap

PakGusu was founded to bring world-class cleanroom technology to Pakistan’s fast-growing pharmaceutical and high-tech sectors, addressing the need for high-quality local manufacturing.

  • Established to reduce reliance on imported cleanroom components.
  • Focus on ISO-classified and GMP-compliant designs.
  • Launched with a mission to deliver safe, efficient environments.

Growth: Local Manufacturing Excellence

We expanded our footprint in Lahore, equipping our facility with advanced machinery to produce insulated panels, doors, and cleanroom accessories locally.

  • Commissioned dedicated production lines for cleanroom panels.
  • Successful delivery of projects for major pharma and hospital clients.
  • Built a multi-disciplinary engineering and validation team.

Partnership: Global Standards, Local Service

Our strategic partnership with GUSU Purification (China) combines global cleanroom engineering expertise with our local manufacturing capabilities.

  • Access to advanced materials and proven GMP designs.
  • Shared expertise for complex ISO 5-8 environments.
  • Offering globally benchmarked solutions with local after-sales support.

COMPLIANCE PILLARS

Standards at the Core of Every Project

We combine process discipline, documented procedures, and real-world experience to ensure that every installation stands up to regulatory scrutiny and customer audits.

  • ISO-based quality management system with controlled procedures.
  • Risk-based design reviews and documented verification plans.

Aligned with Global ISO Frameworks

Our cleanroom designs, layouts, and documentation are structured around key ISO and EN standards to ensure global acceptance.

  • ISO 14644 series for cleanroom classification and monitoring.
  • ISO 14698 contamination control and biocontamination.
  • ISO 9001-based processes governing design, production, and service.

Documented and Verifiable Compliance

Third-party assessments, internal audits, and customer approvals verify that our systems and procedures remain in a state of control.

  • Independent calibration and validation partners where required.
  • Regular internal audits mapped to ISO 9001 and GMP expectations.
  • Project-specific dossiers: FAT, SAT, IQ/OQ, and as-built records.

Quality by Design, Not by Inspection

We embed quality early in the lifecycle, focusing on prevention, transparency, and continuous improvement rather than rework.

  • Structured design reviews and risk assessments at each stage.
  • Standardized test protocols and acceptance criteria.
  • Closed-loop feedback from commissioning, operations, and audits.
