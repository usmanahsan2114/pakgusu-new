About PakGusu

Cleanroom Manufacturer in Pakistan

PakGusu Technology (Pvt) Ltd is a leading cleanroom manufacturer in Pakistan, delivering turnkey modular cleanroom solutions for pharmaceutical, healthcare, electronics, food, and research industries. Backed by our strategic partnership with GUSU Purification (China), we combine global cleanroom expertise with local manufacturing and on-ground support.

Turnkey Design & Build
We offer end-to-end solutions including design, manufacturing, installation, and validation of cleanroom systems tailored to your specific needs.
ISO-Classified Environments
Our solutions are engineered to meet ISO 14644-1 cleanroom classes (ISO 5–8) and GMP guidelines, ensuring regulatory compliance.
Local Manufacturing Support
Benefit from local production for faster lead times, cost efficiency, and dedicated after-sales support for maintenance and upgrades.
Manufactured in Pakistan
