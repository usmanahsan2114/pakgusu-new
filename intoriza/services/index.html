Turnkey Services

Turnkey Cleanroom Services – From Design to Validation

PakGusu provides end-to-end cleanroom services that take your project from concept to a fully operational, validated facility. Our turnkey approach reduces project risk, simplifies coordination, and accelerates time-to-operation.

  • Planning & Design
  • Cleanroom Construction
  • Installation & Commissioning

We start with an in-depth understanding of your process, regulatory requirements, and space constraints. Our engineering team then defines required ISO cleanroom classes and GMP grades, develops layouts for rooms, airlocks, and support areas, and prepares detailed drawings and technical specifications.

  • ISO Cleanroom Class Definition
  • 3D Modeling & Layouts
  • Airflow Simulation

After design approval, PakGusu handles construction using our modular cleanroom components. Our project managers keep construction on schedule while ensuring every joint, seal, and finish meets quality standards. We coordinate everything from panel installation to HVAC integration.

  • Modular Panel Installation
  • Cleanroom Doors & Windows
  • HVAC & Ducting Integration

During installation and commissioning, we focus on fine-tuning the environment. We balance airflows to achieve target air changes per hour (ACH), verify temperature, humidity, and pressure differentials, and run performance tests to ensure your cleanroom meets all certification requirements.

  • Air Balancing & Commissioning
  • Particle Counting & Smoke Tests
  • Qualification Documentation
