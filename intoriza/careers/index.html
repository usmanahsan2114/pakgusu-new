Build the future of clean environments with us.

Join PakGusu and help build the next generation of cleanroom facilities in Pakistan. We’re looking for engineers, technicians, and professionals who are passionate about quality, innovation, and customer success.

We operate like a product team inside an engineering company: small, empowered squads that own problems end-to-end. You’ll collaborate directly with people in design, manufacturing and field operations.
