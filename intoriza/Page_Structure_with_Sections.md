# Page Structure with Sections

## Home
- **Hero Section:** A full-width hero with tagline, supporting text, and a prominent Call-To-Action (CTA) button.
- **Company Overview:** Brief introduction to PakGusu, highlighting experience and capabilities.
- **Product Highlights:** Grid or carousel showcasing main product categories with images and short descriptions.
- **Services Overview:** Summary of key services offered, each with an icon and a brief blurb.
- **Industries Served:** Section featuring the industries (sectors) PakGusu caters to, possibly with industry icons or images.
- **Call-To-Action Banner:** A concluding banner inviting visitors to get in touch or request a quote.

## About (Section Landing)
- **About Overview:** Introduction to PakGusu’s identity and mission, with a brief summary of the company.
- **Subpage Teasers:** Links or cards for "About Pak Gusu", "About GUSU China", and "Cleanroom Classifications", each with a short description or image.

## About Pak Gusu
- **Company Background:** History and overview of PakGusu in Pakistan (founding, mission, vision).
- **Mission & Vision:** Statement of the company’s mission and vision for cleanroom solutions.
- **Facilities & Team:** Information about the Lahore manufacturing facility and team expertise, possibly with photos.
- **Quality & Compliance:** Highlights of adherence to GMP standards and quality certifications.

## About GUSU China
- **GUSU Overview:** Background of GUSU in China, its experience (years in industry) and global presence.
- **Expertise & Technology:** Outline of GUSU’s technical strengths, innovations, and contributions to cleanroom tech.
- **Partnership:** Explanation of the collaboration between PakGusu and GUSU China and its benefits.

## Cleanroom Classifications
- **Introduction to Cleanrooms:** Definition of cleanrooms and why classification matters.
- **Classification Standards:** Breakdown of different cleanroom standards (e.g., ISO 14644-1 classes, Federal Standard 209E) with brief explanations.
- **Cleanroom Classes Details:** Descriptions of various cleanroom class levels and their typical applications or requirements, possibly in a list or table format.

## Products (Overview)
- **Product Range Introduction:** A brief intro emphasizing the variety and quality of PakGusu’s cleanroom products.
- **Product Category List:** A display of all product categories (Panels, Windows, Doors, etc.) with an image and short description for each, linking to their pages.
- **Quality Assurance:** A note on product quality standards, materials, and compliance (e.g., fire resistance, hygiene).
- **CTA:** Encouragement to explore product pages or contact for more info.

## Clean Room Panels (Product Page)
- **Product Overview:** Description of clean room wall and ceiling panels, their construction and unique features.
- **Specifications/Features:** Key technical specs (materials, insulation, thickness) and features (thermal insulation, easy cleaning) in list form.
- **Applications:** Common uses of clean room panels in various industries.
- **Product Gallery:** Images or diagrams of panel types, joint details, etc.
- **CTA:** Link or button to inquire or request a quote for panels.

## Windows (Product Page)
- **Product Overview:** Description of cleanroom windows (flush design, double-glazed, etc.) and their role in cleanrooms.
- **Specifications/Features:** Key features like tempered glass, silicone-sealed frames, and easy maintenance.
- **Applications:** Typical placement (cleanroom viewing windows, pass-throughs) and industries using them.
- **Product Gallery:** Photos or diagrams of cleanroom window installations.
- **CTA:** Invitation to inquire about cleanroom windows.

## Doors (Product Page)
- **Product Overview:** Description of cleanroom doors (e.g., airtight steel doors, swing/sliding types).
- **Specifications/Features:** Features such as smooth surfaces, self-closing mechanisms, interlock compatibility.
- **Applications:** Use cases like labs, operation theaters, production areas requiring sealed doors.
- **Product Gallery:** Images of various door models in cleanrooms.
- **CTA:** Invitation to inquire about cleanroom doors.

## Transfer Window (Product Page)
- **Product Overview:** Explanation of transfer windows (pass-through chambers) for materials transfer without contamination.
- **Specifications/Features:** Features like interlocking doors, UV sterilization (if any), stainless steel interior.
- **Applications:** How they are used in pharmacies, labs, and production lines to pass materials safely.
- **Product Gallery:** Images of transfer windows/pass-through boxes.
- **CTA:** Invitation to inquire about transfer windows.

## Aluminum Profile (Product Page)
- **Product Overview:** Description of aluminum profiles used in cleanroom construction (framing for panels, coving, etc.).
- **Specifications/Features:** Details on profile types, anodized finish, strength and modularity.
- **Applications:** Instances where profiles are used (wall joints, ceiling grids) across projects.
- **Product Gallery:** Images or cross-section diagrams of profiles.
- **CTA:** Invitation to inquire about aluminum profile products.

## Clean LED Lights (Product Page)
- **Product Overview:** Description of cleanroom-compliant LED lighting (low particulate, sealed fixtures).
- **Specifications/Features:** Key attributes like high lux output, energy efficiency, dust-proof design.
- **Applications:** Use in cleanroom ceilings for various industries.
- **Product Gallery:** Photos of LED light panels and their installation in cleanrooms.
- **CTA:** Invitation to inquire about cleanroom LED lights.

## Services (Overview)
- **Services Introduction:** Brief overview emphasizing PakGusu’s end-to-end cleanroom services.
- **Service List:** Summary of each service (Planning & Design, Construction, Installation, After-Sales) with icons and a sentence describing each.
- **Approach/Workflow:** A high-level outline of how a project moves from design to after-care (could include a process diagram).
- **CTA:** Encouragement to learn more about services or to request a consultation.

## Planning and Design (Service Page)
- **Service Overview:** Description of the planning and design service for cleanrooms (layout design, engineering drawings, compliance planning).
- **Process & Deliverables:** Outline of steps (consultation, custom design, 3D modeling) and deliverables (layouts, airflow plans).
- **Expert Team:** Highlight of the experienced design team and their knowledge of regulatory standards.
- **CTA:** Prompt to start planning a cleanroom project with PakGusu’s team.

## Clean Room Construction (Service Page)
- **Service Overview:** Description of the cleanroom construction service (modular construction, project management).
- **Process & Quality:** Key steps (site preparation, panel installation, HVAC integration) and quality checks (testing for integrity, certification).
- **Expertise:** Emphasis on skilled construction crew and meeting deadlines while maintaining standards.
- **CTA:** Prompt to initiate a cleanroom construction project inquiry.

## Installation (Service Page)
- **Service Overview:** Description of installation services (setup of cleanroom equipment, HVAC systems, and validation).
- **Scope:** What installation covers – from cleanroom components to electrical and filtration systems.
- **Efficiency:** Emphasis on minimal downtime and proper calibration during installation.
- **CTA:** Prompt to contact PakGusu for installation support.

## After Sale Services (Service Page)
- **Service Overview:** Description of after-sales support including maintenance, repairs, and periodic validation of cleanrooms.
- **Offerings:** List of after-sale offerings such as filter replacement, environmental monitoring, staff training, and emergency repairs.
- **Reliability:** Assurance of prompt support to keep client facilities compliant and operational.
- **CTA:** Invitation to schedule maintenance or inquire about service contracts.

## Sectors (Overview)
- **Industries Introduction:** Brief intro about the various industries PakGusu serves and the importance of cleanrooms in each.
- **Sector List:** Grid or list of industries (Pharma, Hospital, etc.) each with an image and short blurb, linking to their dedicated pages.
- **CTA:** Invitation for companies in any industry to contact for tailored cleanroom solutions.

## Pharmaceutical / Nutraceutical (Sector Page)
- **Sector Overview:** Discussion of cleanroom needs in pharma and nutraceutical production (stringent GMP requirements, sterile conditions).
- **PakGusu’s Solutions:** How PakGusu designs and builds facilities for this sector (e.g., modular cleanrooms for tablet manufacturing, etc.).
- **Compliance:** Emphasis on meeting pharmaceutical industry regulations (FDA, GMP standards).
- **CTA:** Prompt for pharmaceutical companies to consult on cleanroom projects.

## Hospital (Sector Page)
- **Sector Overview:** Explanation of cleanroom usage in hospitals (operating rooms, ICU, isolation wards needing controlled environments).
- **PakGusu’s Solutions:** How PakGusu addresses hospital clean spaces (air filtration systems, anti-bacterial surfaces, etc.).
- **Compliance:** Notes on healthcare standards and infection control measures followed.
- **CTA:** Prompt for healthcare facilities to reach out for cleanroom solutions.

## Food Industry (Sector Page)
- **Sector Overview:** Outline of cleanroom or hygienic room needs in food processing (preventing contamination in food and beverage production).
- **PakGusu’s Solutions:** Description of clean, temperature-controlled environments for food plants, adhering to HACCP and safety standards.
- **Benefits:** How cleanroom conditions improve product shelf life and safety in food industry.
- **CTA:** Prompt for food producers to contact PakGusu for contamination-controlled environments.

## Electronics (Sector Page)
- **Sector Overview:** Cleanroom importance in electronics and semiconductor manufacturing (dust-free, static-free environments for microchips, etc.).
- **PakGusu’s Solutions:** How PakGusu provides ultra-clean environments (HEPA filtration, anti-static materials) for electronics assembly and fabrication.
- **Standards:** Mention of required cleanliness levels (e.g., ISO Class 5 or 6 for certain electronics manufacturing).
- **CTA:** Prompt for electronics firms to inquire about bespoke cleanroom setups.

## Laboratories (Sector Page)
- **Sector Overview:** Need for cleanrooms in laboratories and R&D (controlled environment for experiments, biotech research, etc.).
- **PakGusu’s Solutions:** How PakGusu builds modular labs (clean benches, laminar flow units, etc.) to maintain sterile conditions for research and quality control labs.
- **Flexibility:** Emphasis on modular design allowing future expansion or reconfiguration in lab settings.
- **CTA:** Prompt for research institutions to collaborate on laboratory cleanroom design.

## Medical / Surgical Devices (Sector Page)
- **Sector Overview:** Requirements of cleanrooms in medical device manufacturing (strict particulate control to ensure product sterility and quality).
- **PakGusu’s Solutions:** Description of facilities provided (clean assembly lines, packaging rooms with environmental control) to meet ISO 13485 or similar standards.
- **Quality Assurance:** How PakGusu’s cleanrooms help in achieving zero contamination for sensitive device production.
- **CTA:** Prompt for medical device manufacturers to consult on clean production spaces.

## Contact
- **Contact Information:** Company contact details (address, phone, email) and a simple contact form for inquiries.
- **Map/Location:** Embed or link to a map of the company’s location in Lahore for easy finding.
- **Business Hours:** (If applicable) mention of availability or hours of operation.
- **Inquiry Form:** Fields for name, email, phone, and message, enabling visitors to request quotes or ask questions.
