## Cleanroom Classifications (About Page)

# Understanding Cleanroom Classifications
Cleanrooms are classified by the cleanliness of their air, specifically by the concentration of particles per volume of air. Different industries require different levels of air purity. Here we explain the major **cleanroom classification standards**:

## ISO 14644-1 Cleanroom Standards
The **ISO 14644-1** standard (from the International Standards Organization) is the globally recognized system for cleanroom classification. It defines cleanroom classes ISO Class 1 (most stringent) through ISO Class 9 (least stringent) based on the maximum number of particles allowed per cubic meter of air at specific particle sizes. For example:
- **ISO Class 5:** Often required for pharmaceutical sterile manufacturing, allows only up to 3,520 particles of ≥0.5 µm per cubic meter.
- **ISO Class 7:** Common for less critical clean areas, allows up to 352,000 particles of ≥0.5 µm per cubic meter.

These ISO classes help facilities determine if their cleanroom meets the necessary purity for their processes (e.g., microelectronics typically need ISO 5 or 6, while food industries might use ISO 7 or 8).

## Federal Standard 209E (USA)
Before ISO became widespread, the U.S. **Federal Standard 209E** classified cleanrooms by counting particles ≥0.5 µm per cubic foot of air. You might recognize terms like **Class 100** or **Class 10,000**:
- **Class 100** (equivalent roughly to ISO Class 5) means no more than 100 particles ≥0.5 µm per cubic foot.
- **Class 10,000** (roughly ISO Class 7) means no more than 10,000 particles ≥0.5 µm per cubic foot.

While FS 209E has been officially replaced by ISO standards, these terms are still informally used in some industries.

## GMP and Other Standards
In pharmaceutical industries, **GMP cleanroom grades** (A, B, C, D) are used in addition to ISO classes to denote areas for sterile drug manufacturing:
- **Grade A**: High risk operations (akin to ISO Class 5).
- **Grade D**: Less stringent background environments (around ISO Class 8).

Other standards like the older British Standard 5295 have also existed, but they are largely superseded by ISO 14644.

Understanding these classifications is critical because each industry and process will dictate a specific cleanroom class. PakGusu designs and builds cleanrooms to meet the exact classification you need — ensuring that particle counts and cleanliness meet the regulatory requirements for your operations.

**Clean Room Panels** are the core of any modular cleanroom. Our panels feature high-density insulated cores (such as polyurethane or rockwool) with laminated steel surfaces. This provides excellent thermal insulation and a smooth, non-porous finish that is easy to sanitize. Panels are designed for rapid installation and a flush fit, minimizing cracks or crevices where contaminants could accumulate. We offer panels in various thicknesses and coatings (like anti-bacterial or anti-static paint) to suit different environmental needs.

*Key Features:*

- Fire-resistant ratings available for enhanced safety.
- Custom sizes and cut-outs (for windows or equipment) to fit project specifications.

Whether you are constructing an ISO Class 5 pharmaceutical cleanroom or a Class 8 assembly area, our cleanroom panels provide the sturdy, compliant framework you need.

## Windows
Our **cleanroom windows** are specially designed for visibility without compromising cleanliness. These windows are double-glazed and completely flush with the wall panels, preventing any ledges where dust can settle. Each window unit is sealed with silicone gaskets to ensure an airtight fit.

Cleanroom windows allow supervisors and visitors to observe operations from outside the clean area, improving transparency and communication while maintaining strict environmental control.

*Key Features:*
- Tempered or laminated safety glass for durability and shatter resistance.
- Optional integrated blinds or electrochromic glass for light control without dust-gathering curtains.
- Easy installation within our panel system, with coving and clean sealing.

By using PakGusu’s cleanroom windows, facilities get the benefit of natural light and oversight into clean zones while still adhering to **ISO and GMP cleanliness standards**.

## Doors
**Cleanroom doors** from PakGusu are engineered to maintain airtight conditions and withstand frequent usage. We provide both swing doors and sliding doors, constructed with high-quality powder-coated steel or aluminum frames and flush surfaces.

Our doors are equipped with gaskets and automatic drop seals to prevent contaminants from passing through edges. They can be configured with interlock systems – ensuring that when one door in a connected cleanroom opens, others automatically lock if needed (common in airlock entry systems).

*Key Features:*
- Smooth, flush panels with either stainless steel or coated finishes that resist corrosion and are easy to clean.
- Available with viewing windows (flush double-glazed) to see through without opening.
- Hardware options including touchless sensors or foot kick plates for hands-free operation in sensitive areas.

With robust cleanroom doors, your facility can maintain pressure differentials between rooms and prevent cross-contamination, which is essential for environments like pharmaceutical manufacturing or hospital isolation rooms.

## Transfer Window (Pass-Through Chambers)
The **transfer window**, also known as a pass-through box, is a small chamber installed in the wall between a cleanroom and an adjacent space. It allows materials and samples to be passed in and out without needing personnel to enter, thereby reducing foot traffic and contamination risk.

PakGusu’s pass-through chambers are built with stainless steel interiors and smooth surfaces. They come with interlocking doors: only one side can open at a time, which preserves the cleanroom pressure and cleanliness.

*Key Features:*
- Mechanical or electronic interlock systems for safety and contamination control.
- UV light sterilization option inside the chamber for additional decontamination between uses.
- Available in various sizes (small pass-through boxes for documents or tools, up to larger chambers for carts).

Using our transfer windows helps maintain your **cleanroom’s integrity** whenever objects need to move in or out, critical for laboratories and production areas.

## Aluminum Profile Systems
Our **aluminum profiles** are the backbone of assembling modular cleanrooms. These profiles (tracks, channels, covings, and ceiling suspension grids) are made from high-grade aluminum alloys with an anodized finish for corrosion resistance.

Profiles are designed to integrate panels, windows, and doors seamlessly, ensuring a flush interior finish. Cove profiles create curved transitions between walls and ceilings or floors, eliminating sharp corners where contaminants can gather and easing cleaning.

*Key Features:*
- Strong yet lightweight framework that supports cleanroom structures securely.
- Precise extrusions that allow quick, secure locking of panels and accessories.
- Sealed and smooth surface profiles to maintain cleanliness and aesthetics.

By using standardized aluminum profiles, PakGusu cleanrooms are both robust and reconfigurable, allowing future modifications or expansions with minimal disruption.

## Clean LED Lights
Lighting in cleanrooms must be bright, efficient, and free of particulate contamination. Our **cleanroom LED light fixtures** are designed to be flush-mounted into ceilings or walls, with sealed housings that prevent any dust infiltration or insect ingress.

These LED panels provide high lumen output to properly illuminate workspaces, while emitting minimal heat (important for climate-controlled environments). They have long lifespans, reducing maintenance in critical areas.

*Key Features:*
- Sealed design with tempered glass or acrylic covers that sit flush with ceiling panels.
- Energy-efficient LEDs that meet lux level requirements for laboratories and production (e.g., achieving recommended 500+ lux for detailed assembly work).
- Optional dimmable controls or integration with automation systems for adaptive lighting.

Our cleanroom lighting ensures uniform illumination, contributing to both the performance and safety of your cleanroom operations, from pharmaceutical compounding labs to microelectronics manufacturing lines.

## Hospital & Healthcare
In hospitals and healthcare settings, controlling infection and contamination is literally life-saving. PakGusu builds modular cleanrooms and controlled environments such as:
- **Operating Theaters (Operation Rooms):** with HEPA-filtered laminar flow ceilings to maintain ultra-clean air during surgeries (targeting ISO Class 5 at the patient area).

- **Pharmacy Cleanrooms:** for compounding sterile medications (meeting USP <797> standards and ISO Class 7 or better).

We use anti-microbial materials and seamless finishes to ensure that every surface in these rooms can be thoroughly disinfected. Our healthcare cleanrooms contribute to lower infection rates, safer surgeries, and compliance with health regulations.

## Food & Beverage Industry
The food industry benefits from cleanroom-like hygienic rooms especially in processes like dairy product filling, ready-to-eat food packaging, and beverage bottling. While not sterile, these environments must be extremely clean to avoid contamination that could spoil products or cause illness.

PakGusu provides:

- Anti-static (ESD-safe) flooring and work surfaces to prevent electrostatic discharge that could harm electronic components.
- Precise environmental controls not just for particulate, but also for temperature and humidity, which can be critical in processes like lithography or battery manufacturing.

Our electronic industry clients see improved product yields and fewer defects thanks to the pristine conditions maintained by PakGusu cleanrooms.
