# Services Page Design Documentation

**URL:** `/services/`
**Source File:** `intoriza/services/index.html`
**Parent Template:** N/A (Custom Layout)

## Page Overview
Details the turnkey cleanroom services offered by PakGusu, from initial planning and design through to validation and ongoing support.

## Section-by-Section Design

### 1. Header
- **Type:** Standard Global Header
- **Includes:** Dark Mode Toggle. Header is always Dark Blue (#004685) with Light Blue (#29afe3) accents. See [Dark Mode](../dark-mode.md).

### 2. Page Title / Breadcrumb
- **Type:** Inner Page Banner (`.wt-bnr-inr`)
- **Content:** "Turnkey Cleanroom Solutions" title and breadcrumb.

### 3. Our Services Overview
- **Layout:** Text Block
- **Content:** Introduction to the end-to-end service model.

### 4. Service Cards Section
- **Layout:** 4-Column Grid (or 2x2 on tablet)
- **Content:**
    - **Planning & Design:** Conceptual layouts, airflow analysis, classification.
    - **Cleanroom Construction:** Modular build with local components.
    - **Installation & Commissioning:** Equipment setup, balancing, testing.
    - **Validation & Certification:** IQ/OQ/PQ protocols, compliance documentation.
- **Styling:** Cards with icons and descriptions (`.wt-icon-box-wraper`).

### 5. Process Timeline (Optional)
- **Layout:** Horizontal or vertical timeline.
- **Content:** Steps from initial consultation to project handover.

### 6. Call-to-Action (CTA)
- **Layout:** Full-width banner.
- **Content:** "Request a Quote" or "Contact Us" button.

### 7. Footer
- **Type:** Standard Global Footer (Dark Blue #004685, Light Blue #29afe3 accents, white text)

## Scripts
- **Waypoints:** For scroll animations.
