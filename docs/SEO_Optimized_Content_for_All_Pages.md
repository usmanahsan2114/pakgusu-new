## Services – Turnkey Cleanroom Solutions (`/services`)

# Turnkey Cleanroom Services – From Design to Validation

PakGusu provides **end-to-end cleanroom services** that take your project from concept to a fully operational, validated facility. Our turnkey approach reduces project risk, simplifies coordination, and accelerates time-to-operation.

### Planning & Design

We start with an in-depth understanding of your process, regulatory requirements, and space constraints. Our engineering team then:

- Defines required **ISO cleanroom classes** and GMP grades
- Develops layouts for rooms, airlocks, and support areas
- Designs airflow patterns, pressure cascades, and HVAC integration
- Prepares detailed drawings and technical specifications

The result is a **compliance-driven cleanroom design** that supports efficient workflows and future expansion.

### Cleanroom Construction

After design approval, PakGusu handles construction using our modular cleanroom components:

- Installation of cleanroom panels, doors, and windows
- Construction of ceilings, covings, and structural supports
- Integration of HVAC systems, filtration, and ducting
- Coordination with electrical and mechanical contractors

Our project managers keep construction on schedule while ensuring every joint, seal, and finish meets quality standards.

### Installation & Commissioning

During installation and commissioning, we focus on fine-tuning the environment:

- Setting up HEPA filter housings, diffusers, and return grilles
- Balancing airflows to achieve target air changes per hour (ACH)
- Verifying temperature, humidity, and pressure differentials
- Running performance and integrity tests (e.g., particle counts, smoke tests)

We train your team in cleanroom usage and provide documentation needed for qualification and audits.

### After-Sales Support & Re-Certification

Cleanroom performance must be maintained throughout its life. PakGusu offers:

- Scheduled maintenance for filters, seals, and equipment
- Emergency repair services to minimize downtime
- Periodic cleanroom re-certification and environmental monitoring
- Support for modifications and expansions as your facility grows

With PakGusu as your **cleanroom partner**, you gain a single point of responsibility and a team committed to long-term performance.
